OUR MISSION

At AdsInMovies, our mission is to make cinema advertising a smart, affordable, and high-impact choice for businesses across India. We believe that advertising during the intermission is far more than a routine commercial break—it is an opportunity to engage an audience that is already seated, attentive, and emotionally connected to the cinematic experience.

Our endeavour is to create awareness among brands, businesses, and marketers about the cost-effectiveness and reach of cinema advertising, especially intermission advertising, which can deliver meaningful visibility without demanding the budgets traditionally associated with mass media. With our pan-India cinema network, we aim to connect brands with the right audiences across cities, towns, and markets. We are committed to offering transparent, flexible, and pocket-friendly advertising solutions that deliver visibility, recall, and value.

Our mission is simple: to make cinema advertising accessible to every business and make every advertising rupee count.

OUR VISION

Our vision at AdsInMovies is to become India's most trusted and preferred partner for cinema advertising, transforming the way businesses perceive and use advertising in movie theatres. We envision a future where cinema advertising is not considered an expensive privilege reserved for large brands, but a powerful, affordable, and measurable medium accessible to businesses of every size.

By leveraging the captive attention of cinema audiences and our extensive pan-India network, we aspire to create meaningful connections between brands and consumers at a time when distractions are minimal and engagement is high. We seek to educate the market about the often-overlooked cost-effectiveness of intermission advertising and demonstrate how the right message, delivered to the right audience, can create strong brand awareness and recall.

Our vision is to build AdsInMovies into a nationwide platform where affordability meets impact, cinema meets commerce, and every screen becomes an opportunity for a brand to be remembered.
